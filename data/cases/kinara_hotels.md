# Kinara Hotels: Owning Hotels or Lending the Name?

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Shalini Kapur, CEO of Kinara Hotels, had a target from her board: 100 hotels by 2030. She had to recommend how to get there at the October 2026 board meeting.

## Background

Kinara owns 24 mid-premium hotels. FY2026 revenue was ₹1,240 crore and revenue per available room was ₹5,800. Debt is ₹1,700 crore. Building a new owned hotel costs about ₹180 crore and takes four years.

## The franchise pilot

In 2025 Kinara signed 6 franchise and management contracts with owners who used the Kinara name.

| Franchise pilot | Value |
|---|---|
| Hotels | 6 |
| Kinara's fee | 4% of revenue plus 8% of operating profit |
| Kinara's capital spent | almost none |
| Average guest rating, owned hotels | 4.5 out of 5 |
| Average guest rating, franchise hotels | 4.1 out of 5 |

## The options

1. **Franchise-led growth:** sign about 70 franchise and management contracts by 2030.
2. **Sell and lease back:** sell 5 owned hotels to an investment trust for ₹900 crore, keep running them, and use the money to cut debt.
3. **Keep owning:** build or buy 10 more hotels with new borrowing.

## Other facts

International chains offer owners similar franchise terms and stronger loyalty programmes. Kinara's loyalty programme has 1.2 million members, who make up 38% of bookings.

## Questions for the board

Which route should Kinara choose, and how would it protect the guest experience?
