# Pragati Bank: Closing Branches in a Digital Age

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Sanjay Mathur, CEO of Pragati Bank, had to decide what to do with the bank's loss-making branches. The board would meet in October 2026.

## Background

Pragati is a mid-sized private bank with 640 branches. 74% of customer transactions now happen digitally. 120 branches, mostly in semi-urban towns, lose money; each costs about ₹1.1 crore a year to run, ₹132 crore a year in total.

## What the branches hold

| The 120 branches | Value |
|---|---|
| Deposits | ₹6,800 crore |
| Share in low-cost current and savings accounts | 44% |
| Customers over 55 | 58% |
| Staff | 900 |
| Estimated deposits lost if branches close | 15% |

## The options

1. **Close** all 120 branches and redeploy staff.
2. **Convert** them to small "digital help points" with 2 staff each, costing ₹30 lakh a year per point.
3. **Keep** them and push them to sell more loans and insurance.

## Other facts

The regulator expects banks to open 25% of new branches in unbanked rural areas, and watches closures in such areas. Replacing lost low-cost deposits with other funding would cost about 2.5 percentage points more. The employees' association has asked for no job losses.

## Questions for the board

What should Pragati do with these branches, and how would it protect customers and staff?
