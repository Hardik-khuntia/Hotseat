# Thali Theory: Leaving the Delivery Apps?

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Pallavi Nair, founder of Thali Theory, had to decide how to make the company profitable. The board would meet in October 2026.

## Background

Thali Theory runs 14 food brands from 60 kitchens in 5 cities, selling only through two large delivery apps. FY2026 revenue was ₹310 crore, with a loss of ₹28 crore.

| Economics | Value |
|---|---|
| Delivery-app commission | 26% of order value |
| Average order value | ₹380 |
| Customers who reorder within 30 days | 18% |
| Share of orders from the top 3 brands | 71% |

## The options

1. **Own app:** spend ₹25 crore on an app and marketing, offering prices 10% lower than on delivery apps. Rivals that tried this got about 15% of orders through their own apps after a year.
2. **Dine-in:** open 30 small restaurants for the top 3 brands at ₹1.2 crore each, ₹36 crore in total.
3. **Simplify:** close 11 weak brands, keep the top 3 on delivery apps, and cut kitchen costs.

## Other facts

The delivery apps also run their own private-label food brands. Customers mostly find Thali Theory through searches on the apps, not by its brand names.

## Questions for the board

What should Thali Theory do, and in what order?
