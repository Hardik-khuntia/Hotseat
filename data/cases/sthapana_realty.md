# Sthapana Realty: Luxury Towers or Affordable Homes?

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Rajiv Hegde, Managing Director of Sthapana Realty, had to decide what to build on an 18-acre plot in north Bengaluru, bought for ₹290 crore. The board would meet in October 2026.

## Background

Sthapana has debt of ₹1,100 crore at an interest rate of 12.5%. Bengaluru has about 21 months of unsold homes on the market. Under the real-estate regulator's rules, Sthapana must deliver homes on the dates it promises buyers or pay penalties.

## The options

| | Luxury | Affordable |
|---|---|---|
| Homes | 900 | 3,200 |
| Average price | ₹2.4 crore | ₹55 lakh |
| Profit margin | 28% | 14% |
| Expected sales per month | 12 | 65 |
| Construction time | 4 years | 3 years |

Affordable buyers can get a government interest subsidy on home loans, which helps sales. Luxury buyers often pay more upfront, which reduces borrowing.

## A mix

A mix of 300 luxury and 2,000 affordable homes is possible, but buyers of luxury homes may not want to share a site with affordable housing.

## Other facts

A metro line opening near the site in 2028 is expected to lift prices. A competitor launched a luxury project 3 km away in 2025 and has sold only 30% of it.

## Questions for the board

What should Sthapana build, and how should it manage its debt while it builds?
