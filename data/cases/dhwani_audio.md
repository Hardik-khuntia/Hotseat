# Dhwani Audio: From Online to Showroom Shelves

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Neil Kapadia, CEO of Dhwani Audio, had an offer from ElectroBazaar, a large electronics retail chain. He had to recommend whether to accept it at the October 2026 board meeting.

## Background

Dhwani sells earbuds, headphones and speakers. FY2026 revenue was ₹520 crore, with 88% from online marketplaces. Returns are 14% of online sales, and the average online order is ₹1,300. Marketplace price wars have cut gross margin from 38% to 29% in two years.

## The offer

| ElectroBazaar terms | Value |
|---|---|
| Stores | 160, across 40 cities |
| Retailer margin | 18% |
| Credit period | 45 days |
| Demo units and display stands | paid by Dhwani, about ₹6 crore |
| Average purchase by store shoppers in this category | ₹2,900 |
| Returns in stores | about 3% |

## The options

1. Accept ElectroBazaar's offer.
2. Open 25 of Dhwani's own experience stores at ₹80 lakh each.
3. Stay online and launch a premium product line.

## Other facts

Global brands dominate store shelves and pay for prime display spots. Store shoppers like to try products before buying, especially above ₹2,000. Dhwani's working capital is already stretched; it pays suppliers in 30 days.

## Questions for the board

Should Dhwani go offline, and how?
