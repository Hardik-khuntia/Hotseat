# Kavach Insurance: Tiny Policies Inside Other Apps?

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Anita Desai, CEO of Kavach General Insurance, had to decide whether to scale up "embedded" insurance, sold at checkout inside other companies' apps. The board would meet in October 2026.

## Background

Kavach is a mid-sized general insurer with FY2026 premiums of ₹2,800 crore, mostly from motor and health insurance. Its combined ratio (claims plus expenses, divided by premium) is 104%, meaning it loses money on underwriting.

## The proposal

Offer small policies inside partner apps: trip-delay cover in a travel app, phone-screen cover in a shopping app, and accident cover in a ride app.

| Embedded insurance plan, year 1 | Value |
|---|---|
| Policies | 9 million |
| Premium per policy | ₹19 to ₹99 |
| Expected claims, as share of premium | 22% |
| Commission demanded by partners | 45% of premium |
| Technology cost | ₹35 crore |

## Concerns

The regulator has banned pre-ticked insurance boxes at checkout and has warned insurers about mis-selling tiny policies that customers don't know they bought. In a 2025 pilot, 41% of customers who made no claim did not remember buying the cover. Claims for small policies are often rejected because customers don't know how to claim.

## Questions for the board

Should Kavach scale embedded insurance, and if so, on what terms and with which safeguards?
