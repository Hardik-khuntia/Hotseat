# Mitra Finance: Lending to Gig Workers at Scale

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Priya Menon, CEO of Mitra Finance, had a term sheet on her desk. ZipRide, a large ride and delivery platform, wanted Mitra to become the only lender offering small loans to its 6 lakh active riders. The deal could grow Mitra's loan book five times in two years. It could also tie Mitra's fate to one partner. The board would meet on 20 October 2026.

## Background

Mitra is a non-bank lender (NBFC) based in Bengaluru with ₹220 crore of equity and a loan book of ₹640 crore. It lends to self-employed people with thin credit histories. Gross NPAs were 3.4% in FY2026, and return on equity was 11%.

## The pilot

Since January 2026, Mitra has lent to ZipRide riders through a pilot in two cities.

| Pilot metric (January to August 2026) | Value |
|---|---|
| Loans disbursed | 12,000 |
| Average loan size | ₹18,000 |
| Tenure | 6 months |
| Annual interest rate | 26% |
| Loans 30+ days overdue | 6.8% (business plan assumed 4%) |
| Overdue rate where repayment was auto-deducted from weekly payouts | 3.1% |
| Riders who took a second loan | 41% |

Riders mainly borrow to repair or buy two-wheelers, or to cover medical bills.

## The ZipRide offer

- Mitra would be the exclusive lender for 3 years, targeting 2 lakh loans in year 1.
- ZipRide shares rider earnings data, so Mitra can lend based on actual weekly income.
- Repayments can be auto-deducted from weekly payouts, if the rider agrees.
- ZipRide takes 1.5% of the loan amount as a fee, and wants the right to end the deal with 90 days' notice.

## Concerns

The regulator's digital lending rules require that loans are paid directly into the borrower's account, all fees are disclosed upfront, and borrowers can withdraw consent for data sharing. A rider union has complained publicly that auto-deductions "leave riders with nothing in a bad week". ZipRide itself is not yet profitable and changed its payout system twice in 2025.

To fund 2 lakh loans, Mitra would need about ₹360 crore of new borrowing. Its lenders want gross NPAs below 4% to keep the current borrowing rate of 11.5%.

## Questions for the board

Should Mitra sign the exclusive deal, sign a narrower deal, or grow without ZipRide? On what conditions?
