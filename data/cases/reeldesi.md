# ReelDesi: Ads, Bundles or Higher Prices?

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Sourav Bhattacharya, CEO of ReelDesi, had to fix the company's economics before its next fundraise. The board would meet in October 2026.

## Background

ReelDesi streams films and shows in Bengali, Marathi and Odia. It has 4.1 million subscribers paying ₹99 a month, but 11% cancel every month. Content costs ₹380 crore a year, 68% of revenue. Its original series in Bengali are its most-watched content.

## The options

| | Option A: free tier with ads | Option B: telecom bundle |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | Free to watch with ads; paid tier stays | Telecom operator includes ReelDesi in its plans |
| Revenue | Ads at about ₹140 per 1,000 views | ₹45 per subscriber per month, paid by the operator |
| Expected users | 12 million free viewers in a year | 2 million new subscribers |
| Main risk | Up to 30% of paying users switch to free | Low price; operator controls the customer |

**Option C:** cut content spending by 25% and focus on the 3 most-watched genres.

## Other facts

National streaming platforms have started adding regional shows. Advertisers pay more for viewers in big cities, but most ReelDesi viewers are in smaller towns. Its investors expect losses to halve within two years.

## Questions for the board

Which option should ReelDesi choose, and what would it measure to know if it's working?
