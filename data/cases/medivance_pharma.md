# Medivance Pharma: Fix, Sell or Pivot the US Business?

*A fictional teaching case written for classroom use. All names and figures are invented.*

## The decision

Nikhil Bhatt, CEO of Medivance Pharma in Ahmedabad, had a buyer's offer for the company's US generics business. He had to recommend to the board in October 2026 whether to accept it.

## Background

Medivance had FY2026 revenue of ₹3,400 crore. US generics make up 46% of revenue but prices there fall about 9% a year. The India branded-medicines business grows 11% a year.

| Business | Share of revenue | EBITDA margin |
|---|---|---|
| US generics | 46% | 13% |
| India branded medicines | 38% | 24% |
| Other export markets | 16% | 15% |

## The warning letter

In 2025 the US regulator issued a warning letter to Medivance's Vadodara plant after an inspection found data-integrity lapses. Until the plant is cleared, 14 product launches are blocked. Fixing it would cost about ₹180 crore and take 18 to 24 months, with no guarantee of clearance.

## The options

1. **Fix and stay:** spend ₹180 crore on remediation and keep the US business.
2. **Sell:** a buyer has offered ₹2,100 crore for the US business, including the Vadodara plant, conditional on 1,200 employees transferring.
3. **Pivot:** fix the plant, then invest ₹600 crore over four years in complex injectables, which face fewer competitors and less price erosion.

## Other facts

The promoter family owns 52% and is reluctant to shrink the company. Analysts value Indian branded businesses at much higher multiples than US generics.

## Questions for the board

Which option creates the most value, and which risks would the board be accepting?
